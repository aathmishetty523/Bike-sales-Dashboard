📌 Project Overview

The Bike Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze customer demographics, purchasing behavior, income, and commute distance.

The dashboard provides insights into which customer segments are more likely to purchase bikes and how factors such as gender, age, education, marital status, region, income, and commute distance relate to bike purchases.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze bike purchasing patterns among customers.
Compare the income of customers who purchased vs. did not purchase bikes.
Identify purchasing trends across different age groups.
Analyze bike purchases based on commute distance.
Understand customer demographics such as:
Gender
Age
Marital Status
Education
Region
Build an interactive dashboard for easy business analysis.
📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

1. Avg Income Per Purchase

Compares the average income of customers based on:

Gender
Bike Purchase Status

Key observation:

Male customers show a higher average income than female customers.
Customers who purchased a bike generally have a higher average income than those who did not.
2. Customer Age Brackets

Analyzes bike purchases across three age groups:

Adolescent
Middle Age
Old

Key observation:

The Middle Age segment has the highest number of customers purchasing bikes.
Bike purchases are considerably lower among the adolescent and older customer groups.
3. Customer Commute Distance

Shows bike purchases according to commute distance:

0–1 Miles
1–2 Miles
2–5 Miles
5–10 Miles
More than 10 Miles

Key observation:

Customers with shorter commute distances represent a significant portion of bike purchases.
Purchase behavior varies across different commute-distance categories.

📈 Key Insights

Some important insights obtained from the dashboard include:

Middle-aged customers account for the highest number of bike purchases compared with adolescent and older customers.
Customers who purchase bikes tend to have a higher average income compared with customers who do not purchase.
Male customers have a higher average income than female customers in the analyzed dataset.
Commute distance has a noticeable relationship with bike purchasing behavior, with shorter commute categories representing substantial purchase activity.
The dashboard allows customer purchasing behavior to be analyzed across different regions, education levels, and marital statuses.
